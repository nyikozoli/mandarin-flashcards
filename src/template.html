        How it works

        598 words from HSK 1, 2 and 3. Each card has the characters, pinyin, English and Hungarian meanings, and example sentences you can listen to.

        Study a card

        • Tap the card to show or hide the meanings and example sentences.
        • Swipe right or press I know it when you know the word. Swipe left or press Still learning when you don't. Skip moves on without marking.
        • Read aloud speaks the word, then its example sentences. The small speaker on each sentence plays just that sentence.
        • Made a mistake? The undo arrow next to the card counter takes back the last answer.

        Choose what to study

        All cards
        Every word in the chosen level, in order. Use the shuffle icon to mix them.
        Due
        Words that are ready for review today. A word you know comes back after 1, 3, 7, 14, 30, 60 and then 120 days; a word you miss comes back the same day.
        Hard, Good, Easy
        When the answer is showing, three buttons say how well you knew it, each with when the card comes back. Good is the same as “I know it”. Easy skips a step, Hard keeps the same wait. Keys: H Hard, → Good, E Easy.
        Learning
        Words you marked “Still learning”.
        New
        Cards you have not answered yet.
        Hardest
        The 20 words you have missed most often.

        The level buttons (All, 1, 2, 3) narrow every mode to one HSK level.

        Practice styles

        汉字 → meaning
        See the Chinese, recall the meaning.
        Meaning → 汉字
        See English and Hungarian, say the Chinese word, then tap to check.
        Listening
        Hear the word, guess it, then tap to see it.
        Multiple choice
        Pick the right meaning from four. A right answer counts as Known.
        汉字 only
        Only the characters show; pinyin, tone colours and audio appear when you tap the card. Use it for words you can say but cannot read yet. A word card's back also has Practice these sentences, which opens just that word's example sentences in the current style.
        Build the sentence
        Sentences only. The translation shows, with the sentence's words shuffled below it: tap them in order (tap a placed word, or press Backspace, to take it back). A right order counts as Known toward recall. Another order can still be correct Chinese, so a mismatch shows the sentence and you mark it yourself.
        Typing the answer
        In Meaning → 汉字 (words) you can type the pinyin and press Enter: tone numbers (xue2sheng) or tone marks (xuésheng) both work, and neutral tones can be left out. A right answer counts as Known. T jumps to the box.
        Similar words
        Words only. The meaning shows (🔊 plays the word); pick the right one among words that look or sound alike, such as 买 mǎi “buy” and 卖 mài “sell”, 他 / 她 / 它, or 大 / 太. Only the about 135 words that have such a twin are in this deck; it counts toward reading.
        Fill the gap
        One of the word's example sentences with the word blanked out, and its translation. Pick the word that fits from four of the same length; keys 1–4 pick. Words only; it counts toward reading.
        Which tone?
        Hear the word, then pick the tone of its first syllable from the four written forms; keys 1–4 pick tones 1–4. Words only. The 21 words whose first syllable is neutral, or changes tone when it is spoken (你好, 可以), come up as normal cards.
        Tap a word
        In any example sentence, and on sentence cards, the words from HSK 1–3 have a dotted underline. Tap one to see its pinyin and meaning; tap elsewhere or press Esc to close it. On a word card the word itself is highlighted instead.
        What's new
        After an update a short notice appears at the top; Settings → What's new lists every version.
        Chinese labels
        Buttons and headings carry their Chinese name (复习 fùxí = Due, 我会了 wǒ huì le = I know it), so you pick up everyday words as you use the app. Settings → Interface labels switches between 中文 + English, English, and 中文 only; hover or long-press a label to see its pinyin.
        Your panda
        The little panda next to today's count lives on your studying. Every answer feeds it a bamboo leaf, your daily goal fills its belly, and all your answers together make it grow from newborn to sage. Tap it to see how it is doing and to give it a name.
        Word of the day
        Each day a useful word you do not know yet appears above the card. “Study it” opens it; ✕ hides it until tomorrow.
        Daily quests
        Three small goals each day, shown in the progress view. Each finished quest gives 30 XP; all three give 50 XP more and a 🧊 streak freeze. You can save two, and a saved freeze keeps your streak alive over a missed day. Every Monday a short recap of last week appears.
        Seals and challenges
        The progress view collects 20 red seals (印章) for milestones such as 100 words, a 7-day streak or a passed exam, each worth 50 XP. Its challenges are a 60-second blitz (as many meanings as you can) and a 20-question exam per HSK level (pass at 80%). Challenges never change your review schedule.
        Character hints
        The back of a word card explains its characters where a well-established explanation exists (about 180 characters): a picture (山 mountain peaks), a combination of meanings (休 a person resting by a tree), or a meaning part plus a sound part (妈 = 女 woman + 马 mǎ for the sound).
        Topics
        The button under the level row picks a topic (food, travel, time…). Every study mode then works inside it, and a topic's seal turns red when you know all its words.
        What you can read
        The progress view counts the example sentences whose HSK words you all know, shows how much of everyday Chinese text your words cover, and suggests the next word to learn. Word frequencies come from wordfreq by Robyn Speer and contributors (CC BY-SA 4.0).
        XP, ranks, combo
        Every answer earns XP (10 for a known card, 3 for one you are still learning, more during a combo). XP raises your rank from 新手 Newcomer to 大师 Master. Three or more right answers in a row show a 连 combo counter on the card.
        Progress per skill
        Reading (Chinese → meaning, 汉字 only, multiple choice), recall (meaning → Chinese) and listening (listening, which tone) each keep their own Known and Learning marks and their own Due list, so a word you can read is not marked as one you can say or hear. Words and sentences also keep their own place in the deck.
        Fading pinyin
        Settings → Hide pinyin on cards I know well. In Chinese → meaning, a card whose next review is a week or more away shows only its characters until you tap it, so the pinyin fades as you learn. Off by default.
        Sentences
        The same four styles for the 777 example sentences, in the lower half of the practice menu. Each sentence is a card of its own with its own progress, and its back shows the word it comes from.

        Reading the card

        • Colours show the tone of each syllable: 1st mā, 2nd má, 3rd mǎ, 4th mà, neutral ma. Turn them off in Settings.
        • The red dashed square around each character is a 米字格 practice grid, the same one used in Chinese exercise books. It helps you see how the character fills its square.

        Progress and goals

        • The pill at the top right shows today’s answers against your daily goal, and your streak 🔥 of days in a row.
        • The chart button opens your progress: words known per level, the last 14 days, and a map of every word. Tap a square to study that word.
        • After every 20 cards you get a short summary.

        Sound

        • Speech uses the Chinese voices on your device. If nothing plays, add a Chinese voice: on iPhone, Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices → Chinese; on Android, the Google text-to-speech settings; on Windows, Settings → Time & language → Speech.
        • In Settings, Test voices hides the voices that do not work on this device. If the start of a word gets cut off, keep Fix cut-off audio start switched on.

        Keyboard

        In the mode and level buttons, the arrow keys move the selection instead.

        → I know it ← Still learning Space Skip S Read aloud ↑ ↓ Show or hide 1–4 Choose an answer H Hard E Easy Esc Close a panel

        Your progress is saved on this device

        • Answers are stored in this browser only. Another phone, computer or browser starts fresh, and clearing your browsing data erases them.
        • To continue on another device, open Settings and use Export progress, then Import progress on the other device.
        • On a phone, open the page in the browser and choose Add to Home Screen to use it like an app. Opened from its web address, the home-screen app also works without internet.
